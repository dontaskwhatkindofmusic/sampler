Every control shows its shortcut in brackets. Shift + letter runs a command; plain letters play samples. ← / → selects a sample without playing it. Field shortcuts focus the value: type or use arrow keys, then Enter to return to playing. Shortcuts pause while editing a field; Esc always stops all audio and leaves the field. Tab moves between controls and Enter activates a focused button. Press ? to open or close these notes. Hold numbers + letter to write steps; press \ to release mouse-selected steps. Shift + Backspace clears the pattern.

Drop one audio file onto any letter to load or replace its sample. The target key highlights before you drop. Files stay in this browser; imports keep the first 12 seconds. You can also select a letter and use “import audio”.

A–Z play · ` + letter record while held (12 seconds max) · Backspace / Delete + letter erase · 1–0 + letter toggle steps · Space play / stop · Shift + 1–4 project · Esc stop everything.

For mouse recording, click record, then a letter, then finish recording. “Record one cycle” counts in four beats and captures one full sequence length. Enable click to hear the metronome; use headphones while recording. “Write live” places played notes on the nearest step while the sequence runs.

Eight quarter-note steps = two bars of 4/4. Step 0 is the tenth step. Inactive steps are kept when shortening a pattern. Number chords depend on your keyboard’s rollover; selecting steps with the mouse is an alternative.

Microphone recordings use compressed audio when supported. Imports are limited to the first 12 seconds and saved as compact mono WAV. Audio is decoded before playing. Actual latency depends on your browser and audio device; wired headphones work best. Recording needs microphone permission and HTTPS or localhost.

Projects save on this device in this browser. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; export a backup to keep your work. Samples are never uploaded. Command shortcuts are left to your browser.
